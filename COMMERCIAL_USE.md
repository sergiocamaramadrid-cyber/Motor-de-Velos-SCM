Commercial Use and Ethical Considerations

The SCM Framework (Motor de Velos) is released for scientific, academic, and open use.

For commercial applications, users are encouraged to contact the author.

As part of the ethical foundation of this project, it is strongly encouraged that any commercial benefit derived from the use of this framework contributes a 10% donation to the association "Mi Gran Reto".

This association supports people suffering from severe diseases and reflects the human purpose behind this work.

This request is not a legal restriction, but an ethical commitment aligned with the spirit of the project.
