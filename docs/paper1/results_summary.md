## Interpretation

- A statistically significant correlation is observed between environmental overdensity (δ_mass) and radial persistence of outer-disk kinematics.
- The signal remains after controlling for baryonic mass and SFR.
- Model comparison (ΔAIC) and out-of-sample validation support the inclusion of an environmental term.
- This is a phenomenological result; no specific physical mechanism is assumed.
